Renderer proofs of concept

The same battlefield snapshot, the game's own base and unit models, the same ground painting and scenery, rendered by two engines. Each page has switches for reflections, ambient occlusion, bloom, shadows, rain and plates.

Three.jsLean library. Lighting, shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom from the library; the wet-ground reflection is a small custom shader. Babylon.jsFull engine. Mirror reflections, ambient occlusion, glow and the post-processing pipeline are built in. Development only. Nothing here is loaded by the game or shipped with it.
